Our Story

Built on Passion for Engineering

Receptor Engineering Hub was founded with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between quality engineering services and the everyday needs of Ugandans. Starting as a small electronics and solar installation outfit, we quickly grew into a full-service engineering and digital solutions company.

Over the years, we have expanded our capabilities to include security systems, power backup solutions, electric fencing, full-stack web development, and graphics design — all under one roof. Our growth has been driven by the trust of our clients and the dedication of our team.

Today, we serve hundreds of clients across Uganda, from individual homeowners to large organizations, delivering projects that make a real difference.
